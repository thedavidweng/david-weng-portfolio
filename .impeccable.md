## Design Context

### Users
Creative directors, freelance clients, and individuals with refined taste. They expect a highly polished, boundary-pushing, and memorable portfolio experience that stands out from standard templates.

### Brand Personality
1. **Cult/Industrial (邪教氛围/工业风)**
2. **Trendy/High-Impact (潮流/冲击)**
3. **Dark & Dramatic (暗色系/戏剧性)**
The interface must evoke an intense, confident, and avant-garde emotion, resembling high-end editorial or cutting-edge global portfolios. Expect to see typography dominating the viewport, acting as the primary graphic element rather than just conveying information.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone:** Extreme contrast, dramatic, and bold. Archival, brutalist structural elements like prominent indexing (`[01]`, `(01)`), copyright marks (`©`), and metadata should be used stylistically.
- **Palette:** A strict, high-impact Black and Red pairing. "Cult" aesthetic relying on intense reds against deep, absolute blacks. Grays are relegated purely to subtle structural elements or muted text, never competing with the red/black dichotomy.
- **Typography & Scale:** Needs massive, impactful typography to convey the "drama" and "strike". Small touches should be extremely precise.
- **Subpage vs Homepage:** The home page is structurally where it needs to be but lacks some consistency and motion. The Project subpages should push the boundaries of drama and boldness.

### Design Principles
1. **Unapologetic Contrast:** Use scale (massive type vs micro copy) and color (deep black vs aggressive red) to create tension and drama.
2. **Industrial Precision:** Avoid soft "AI" styles (e.g., glassmorphism, glowing purple gradients). Rely on sharp edges, solid blocks of color, and high-fidelity, rigid grid breaking.
3. **Dramatic Pacing:** Utilize motion and dramatic staggered entrances to create a striking first impression when navigating between the home and project pages.
4. **Cult Minimalism:** Restrain the palette to Black and Red. Let the emptiness (white space/black space) do the heavy lifting to make the content feel exclusive and profound.
