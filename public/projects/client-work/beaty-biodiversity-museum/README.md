# Beaty Biodiversity Museum Archive

This archive is organized for museum-facing design work that does not fit the default social-media archive structure.

Folders:
- `covers/`: archive cover images, recommended around `1600x1600`
- `booklet/`: booklet pages or spreads
- `signage/`: signage, exhibition panels, wayfinding, environmental graphics
- `report/`: annual report pages or editorial spreads
- `video/`: video stills, frames, thumbnails, or motion-design captures

Notes:
- Keep semantic metadata in `content/archives/beaty-biodiversity-museum.ts`.
- Use stable descriptive filenames instead of export-generated names
- Images dropped into these folders are auto-scanned into the archive wall at build time
